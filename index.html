02 / Arrange

Build beyond the loop.

ARRANGE is the multitrack song timeline. Place patterns across bars, adjust the song tempo, and build complete sections and variations.

Work in clips.

Add a clip on DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH, PADS or CHOPS, then open CREATE to edit its pattern. Duplicate clips for new sections, or change their length directly in the timeline.

  • + CLIP / EDIT / DUP / DEL: create a clip, open its notes, duplicate it or remove it.
  • Extend or shorten: resize a clip from its edge. Extending a patterned clip repeats the corresponding sequence so the editor matches the new clip length.
  • BPM & transport: set the tempo and use play/stop to audition your arrangement.
  • Active playback: the green position marker appears on clips that are actually playing, rather than every clip.

03 / Create

Write the music.

Select a track and open a clip in CREATE. Tap an empty cell to add a note; tap an existing note to remove it. Drag a note to move it, or drag its right edge to resize it. Generated notes are editable the same way as manually placed notes.

Use OCT −/+, LEN −/+, NOTE DEL and the VELOCITY control to refine your part. The piano-roll timeline and note labels stay visible as you edit, and the editor focuses on the clip’s existing notes.

DRUMS

Sequence eight drum voices, change kits and set note velocity.

BASS

Write basslines or generate them using the shared progression and key.

SYNTH

Write or generate melodies, riffs and chord patterns.

PADS

Layer chords and pads using the same harmonic progression.

Tap any screenshot to view it at full size. Scroll the track selector to CHOPS and SAMPLER.

Composing from a sample? The sampler estimates the source key and the key after pitch shifting. Use the sample-key guide to highlight notes in the BASS, SYNTH and PADS piano rolls. The detected key is an estimate, so check it by ear.
04 / Sampler & Chops

Make samples your own.

Load a sample in SAMPLER, find its rhythmic hits, place or adjust chop markers, and sequence the resulting slices in CHOPS. The Slice Lab combines manual editing with transient detection, BPM/key estimates and optional warping.

01 / Slice Lab

Choose a file with BROWSE, press PLAY to audition it and use the waveform to locate the sections you want. Pinch/zoom or use − ZOOM and + ZOOM for fine adjustments; FULL returns to the full-sample view.

  • SLICE HITS: automatically propose markers at detected musical transients. Adjust boundaries manually to fine-tune your chops.
  • HIT SNAP / GRID: use detected hits and the beat grid as visual and positioning guides; BPM analysis helps align the grid to the audio.
  • WARP: optionally fit rhythmic material toward the project grid while aiming to preserve pitch. Progress appears beneath the waveform. Results depend on the sample and detected/source BPM.
  • PITCH −1 / +1 / RESET: transpose in semitone steps and view estimated original and pitched keys.
  • APPLY CHOPS: commit the edited chop boundaries to the CHOPS track.

02 / Sequence Chops

Switch to CHOPS in CREATE and place triggers for your sample slices. Each lane plays a chop, so you can rearrange a break, vocal or melodic phrase against the other tracks.

  • 16 chop pads/lanes: select and audition slices from your loaded sample.
  • Step editing: tap to add/remove triggers, drag to move them, or adjust their length.
  • Tempo & mix: arrange chops on the same song timeline and control their level in MIX.
Importing your own audio: On iPhone/iPad, put samples in Files → On My iPhone/iPad → zpunk → ImportedSamples (folder name and capitalization matter). On Android, make your audio available through the device’s Music/media library. WAV and MP3 are practical choices; convert FLAC files before importing.
05 / Generate

A starting point in seconds.

Generate editable patterns for DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH and PADS. Hold a track name in CREATE, or use GEN, to open the generator for the selected clip.

Choose the direction.

Select a genre, style, key and length (1, 2 or 4 bars), then press GENERATE. Try NEW VARIATION for another take and edit the notes directly in CREATE.

  • Genres: Boom Bap, House, Techno, Breaks and Funk. FUNK includes Disco, Pocket and Electro styles.
  • HOUSE / 90s: a dedicated 90s-inspired style for more rhythmic basslines, melodies and chords.
  • Shared progressions: choose a chord progression (or the genre default) for BASS, SYNTH or PADS. The selected progression is shared so each part can follow the same harmonic movement, while its generated variation remains independent.
  • Key awareness: select the musical key; progression/chord labels reflect that choice.
  • Variation history: save promising generated results, revisit them and use or delete stored variations.
  • Important: committing a generated pattern replaces the notes of the selected clip. CANCEL leaves them unchanged.
06 / Sound & Mix

Find your sound. Balance the track.

Browse and shape instrument sounds in SOUND, then use the redesigned MIX page to balance the full five-track arrangement.

SOUND

Switch between BASS, SYNTH and PADS and browse the curated ZOR preset collection. Each instrument has its own sound settings.

  • Synth parameters: oscillator, filter and envelope controls such as cutoff, resonance, attack, decay, sustain and release.
  • Effects: available distortion, delay and reverb modules have on/off controls and adjustable parameters.
  • Arpeggiator: shape repeating note patterns where supported.
  • Preset navigation: use the patch arrows to audition different sounds.

MIX

Balance DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH, PADS and CHOPS using five live channel faders. Meters help you see the activity as the arrangement plays.

  • Faders: set each track’s volume; gain extends up to +6 dB.
  • S / M: solo or mute a channel independently.
  • Level meters: monitor signal activity and avoid overloading your mix.
  • Drum channels: adjust the levels of individual kit voices in the mixer’s drum section.
07 / Save & Export

Keep the idea. Take it further.

Use SAVE in the top bar before closing or switching projects. Export WAV audio or MIDI when you want to share an idea or develop it further in another music app.

Keep imported sample files available on the device: projects that refer to those files may need them at reload time.

PROJECTSave your clips, notes, arrangement, instrument settings and references to imported audio.
WAVRender your song as audio to share or listen outside ZPUNK.
MIDIExport note and timing data for continued sequencing in other software.
FILESOn iOS, find exports in the ZPUNK folder in Files; on Android, check the Music folder.
