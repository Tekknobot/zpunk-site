Main Tab

The performance view.

The main tab is the fastest way to begin a track. Drums, sample triggers, chop selection, mixer balance, transport, and tempo stay visible so you can build the core groove without switching screens.

Drums, chops, mixer, and tempo — all in one place.

Think of the main tab as the groove sketchpad. Tap in drums, trigger sample chops, adjust levels, and evolve the loop while it plays in real time.

Drum Sequencer Select a sound from the Drum Picker — Kick, Snare, Clap, Hats, and more — then tap steps in the sequencer to place hits into the pattern.
Velocity Control Hold a step and drag vertically to change its velocity. Each hit supports three levels: low, mid, and high. The visible label, like K for Kick, represents the mid velocity.
Sample Sequencer Choose a slice from the Chop Picker, then place it into the sample lane to trigger chops rhythmically alongside the drums.
Random Beat Generator Long-press Stop to instantly generate a new groove. zpunk rotates between styles such as house and boom-bap to create quick starting patterns.
Transport & Tempo Press Play to audition the loop while editing. The BPM control can be adjusted during playback to explore different timing and groove.
Fast Workflow Drums, samples, mixer levels, and tempo stay visible on a single screen, so ideas can be sketched quickly before moving to synth, chords, or arrangement.

Keys tab for melodic sequencing.

The keys page is where single-note melodies, riffs, and bass patterns get sketched. It pairs a note grid with fast patch browsing so the sound and the notes can evolve together.

Touch-first note grid Tap steps to place notes quickly, then drag where needed for held notes without cluttering the interface.
Patch browsing Use the patch controls to move through synth sounds while keeping the current sequence intact.
Accurate note lengths Adjacent sixteenths stay separate when entered individually, while dragged notes preserve longer held lengths.
Project recall Saved patterns restore more reliably, so melodic ideas come back the way they were written.

A cleaner synth panel with more hardware feel.

The synth page is about direct sound design. Recent work tightened the layout, removed redundant controls, and made patch shaping feel more deliberate on mobile.

OSC control Adjust volume, tune, wave, unison, detune, and harmonic shape directly from the panel.
ADSR by knobs + sliders Shape amp and modulation behavior more clearly, with a compact layout that stays readable on a phone.
Distortion module Use type, drive, and mix to add edge, weight, or saturation when a patch needs more bite.
Stable loaded state When projects load, visible synth values better reflect the current saved sound instead of snapping after interaction.

Chords tab for fast harmonic progressions.

Instead of entering one note at a time, the chords page lets each step trigger a full harmony. It is designed to keep progression writing fast, visual, and playable.

Chord modes Choose between Major, Minor, 7th, and Sus2 to change the feel of the progression instantly.
Root picker Select a root from the pad grid, then place that harmony into the chord sequencer.
Step sequencing Tap the sequencer cells to build a progression that runs in sync with drums, keys, and samples.
Thumb-friendly layout Built for quick input on mobile without the density of a desktop piano-roll editor.

Sampler tab for chop shaping and fast sample performance.

The sampler turns one audio source into a playable bank of slices. You can quickly divide a sample, zoom into the waveform, adjust chop boundaries, and sequence the pieces directly into your beat.

Load + Chop Press Load to import a sample, then enable Chop Mode to divide the audio into 4, 8, or 16 slices. This creates a bank of playable chops.
Touch waveform controls Use two-finger drag to move across the waveform and pinch to zoom for detailed editing when working with tight chops.
Precise chop editing Use the -0.10 / -0.01 / +0.01 / +0.10 controls on screen to trim or extend the selected chop boundary in small time increments. This makes it easy to tighten drum hits or extend musical tails.
Apply + Chop Picker Press Apply to lock the slice layout, then select individual chops from the Chop Picker grid and place them into the sample sequencer.
Pitch per chop Each chop can be pitched independently, letting one sample become bass notes, melodies, textures, or rhythmic variations.
Live preview Tap chops to audition them instantly so you can quickly test slices before placing them into the sequence.

Chain tab for arrangement and live pattern editing.

Chain view turns short loops into structure. It is built for placing patterns across bars, duplicating ideas forward, and continuing to edit while the arrangement plays.

Pattern workflow Use New, Save, and Dup to create or duplicate patterns, then Place them onto bars in the chain.
Dup + Next Quickly carry an idea forward into a new pattern slot so variations are easy to build section by section.
Live edits stick When the chain is running, edits now write into the active pattern more reliably instead of getting lost.
Arrangement flow Choose chain length, place patterns across bars, and expand a loop into a fuller structure without stopping the sketch.
