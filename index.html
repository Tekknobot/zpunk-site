02 / Arrange

Build beyond the loop.

ARRANGE is the song timeline. Each track holds clips you can build into sections, variations and longer arrangements.

Work in clips.

Make a clip, place it on a track, then open CREATE to edit its notes. Move, resize and duplicate clips as your track takes shape.

  • Five tracks: DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH, PADS and CHOPS.
  • Timeline: position clips across bars and build sections.
  • Playback: use the top transport to hear changes as you work.

03 / Create

Write the music.

Choose a track, open a clip and program its pattern. Tap the grid to add or remove notes. Drag an existing note to move it, or drag its right edge to change its length. Use OCT, LEN and VELOCITY to refine the part.

DRUMS

Step-sequence eight drum sounds and change kits.

BASS

Place bass notes and shape their lengths.

SYNTH

Write melodies, riffs and chord voicings.

PADS

Draw long notes for chords and atmosphere.

Tap any screenshot to open it at full size. The track selector scrolls to reveal CHOPS and SAMPLER.

04 / Sampler & Chops

Make samples your own.

Load audio, mark the parts you want, and turn them into a sequence. ZPUNK offers manual slicing, tempo warp and pitch controls.

01 / Slice Lab

Use BROWSE to choose audio. Zoom and pan the waveform, tap to add a marker, then drag it or use the ±10 ms controls for precision.

  • WARP: sync a sample to the song tempo with pitch preservation.
  • PITCH: shift the sample in semitones.
  • APPLY: use your edited slice boundaries.

02 / Sequence Chops

Switch to CHOPS inside CREATE. Tap cells to trigger slices in time with the rest of your song.

  • Up to 16 slices per sample bank.
  • Step editor: add, move or resize triggers.
  • Mix: set CHOPS level with the other tracks.

On Android, BROWSE reads music available through the device's media library; on iOS, sample import is available through Files.

05 / Generate

A starting point in seconds.

Generate editable patterns for DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH and PADS. Hold the track name in CREATE, or tap GEN, to open the generator.

Choose the direction.

Select a genre, style, key, length and variation, then press GENERATE. Make a new variation until something clicks, and edit the result in the note editor.

  • Genres: Boom Bap, House, Techno and Breaks.
  • Musical control: shared key for bass, synth and pads; 1, 2 or 4 bars.
  • Heads-up: generating replaces the notes in the selected clip. CANCEL leaves them unchanged.
06 / Sound & Mix

Find your sound. Balance the track.

Shape individual instruments in SOUND, then use MIX to bring the five tracks together.

SOUND

Switch between BASS, SYNTH and PADS. Browse presets, then sculpt each instrument without changing the other tracks.

  • Filter: cutoff, resonance and drive.
  • Envelope: attack, decay, sustain and release.

MIX

Set DRUMS, BASS, SYNTH, PADS and CHOPS levels while the arrangement plays.

  • M: mute a track.
  • S: solo a track.
  • Sliders: adjust live gain.
07 / Save & Export

Keep the idea. Take it further.

Use SAVE in the top bar to keep working on a project later. Export WAV audio or MIDI when you're ready to share or continue in another music app.
